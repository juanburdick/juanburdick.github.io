# The Scholars' League

The Scholars' League is an erudite camp of scholars, sages, wizards, and the like who have come to the Agate Coast seeking knowledge rather than treasure. Their careful documentation of the local environment and ecology has enabled them to travel further into the peninsula than any other organization, save perhaps the Order of the Sacred Earth.

### Key Individuals
- Named
  - Marian - Gnome lady in silver robes with poofy hair and big glasses. Historian of oral nomadic traditions.
  - Winnifred - Human man in blue robes, gave us rough details of the camp.
  - Henrith Palan - Eldery dwarf in cleric robes. Adheres to Simreth, Day Pantheon Aspect of Strength.
  - Saffron Notwise - Male halfling, historian of ancient history.
  - Lady Lysandra Serrana - A noble human in her late middle age, with graying blonde hair, she is a respected cartographer with a haughty bearing befitting her direct relation with the imperial throne (Prince Marsys). She has a hyperactive assistant named Tilney.
  - Tilney - A middling halfling lady who excels in mapmaking and logistics by virtue of her boundless frenetic energy that doubtless comes from her midafternoon naps. She is the longtime assistant of the haughty Lady Lysandra, much to everyone's surprise given the obvious disdain.
- Unnamed
  - Old straw-blonde haired elf lady, librarian of Antiquities.

### Notes
- Smells like parchment.
- Copy paper is freely available nearly everywhere.
- Tents and robes are color-coded by discipline.
- Passes are renewed by setting a meeting with the secretary for review of a report on your associated research.
- Maintains a contract with the Shining Swords for camp defense.
  - Shining Swords are behind schedule on moving into the Black Fortress and are defaulting on contracts with the League.
