# Broken Gulf Sailing Company

The Broken Gulf Sailing Company is comprised of courageous sailors and is roughly analogous to the real-world East India Trading Co. (less evil though - probably). Their job is more complex and more dangerous than you might expect due to the stellar drift of constellations that would otherwise provide constant guides. Even they do not dare to stray too far from the coastline.

### Key Individuals
- Named
  - None known yet
- Unnamed

  - Older lady who runs a spice shop. Redimir is a regular.
  - Teenager who works at a bag shop. Terrified of Warmbread after being revenge-extorted and telepathically castigated.
